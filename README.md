# AfyaRoot AI  
### AI-Assisted Research on African Herbal Medicine and Immune Health

## About the Project

AfyaRoot AI is a student-led interdisciplinary research initiative focused on exploring the relationship between African herbal medicine, immune health, and artificial intelligence-assisted scientific discovery.

This project brings together students and researchers from different specialties including AI, biological sciences, health research, and technology to collaboratively investigate traditional medicinal knowledge and its potential relevance in modern scientific understanding.

Our mission is to responsibly combine indigenous knowledge, scientific research, and AI-powered analysis to create accessible, organized, and research-driven insights.

---

## What We Are Researching

Our ongoing research focuses on:

- African herbal medicine and traditional medicinal plants
- Immune system support and wellness-related studies
- AI-assisted scientific literature analysis
- Knowledge organization and data discovery
- Biological and healthcare-related research workflows
- Research simulations and computational analysis
- Ethical preservation of indigenous medicinal knowledge

## Research Philosophy

We believe AI can help accelerate scientific discovery while respecting cultural knowledge and ethical research practices.

AfyaRoot AI aims to:
- Promote collaborative scientific learning
- Encourage responsible AI use in research
- Support accessible healthcare knowledge exploration
- Preserve and digitize valuable indigenous insights
- Inspire student innovation in Africa and beyond
